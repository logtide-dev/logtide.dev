Three things in this release: weak passwords are refused, every email gets the LogTide look, and ClickHouse installs stop wearing out their disks.

**Before upgrading:** there are no database migrations. On ClickHouse, the logs and spans tables move to daily partitions once, in the background after the upgrade, and the move needs about the size of those tables in free disk space (without it they stay monthly, with a warning in the logs). The first nightly retention run on ClickHouse or MongoDB can delete a lot of old spans, traces and metrics that were kept forever until now. Existing account passwords keep working.

### Security

- **Stronger account passwords**: a password set on registration, password change, password reset or from the admin user pages needs at least 12 characters and has to pass a strength check (zxcvbn, run on the server and in the browser, nothing sent anywhere). Common passwords, keyboard sequences, repeats and passwords built from the account's email or name are refused; a few unrelated words pass, and there are no uppercase or symbol rules. The forms show a strength meter with the reason a password is weak. Existing passwords keep working until they are changed. A weak `INITIAL_ADMIN_PASSWORD` still creates the first admin, with a warning in the logs.

### Added

- **`DEFAULT_ORG_RETENTION_DAYS`**: optional hosting setting for the retention new organizations start with. Unset keeps the 90 day default.
- **`CLICKHOUSE_INSERT_FLUSH_MS` and `CLICKHOUSE_TRACE_SUMMARY_FLUSH_MS`**: optional settings for the ClickHouse async insert flush windows (defaults 2000 and 10000 ms). `CLICKHOUSE_DAILY_PARTITIONS=false` keeps existing ClickHouse tables on monthly partitions.

### Changed

- **New email design**: every email LogTide sends (alerts, errors, incidents, Sigma detections, monitors, project health, digests, invitations, email verification, password reset, status page notices, notification channel messages) now looks like the app: the status as a colored label with its word, the key number large, rule and service details as `key=value` lines, the app's violet button. Emails follow the reader's mail app theme: light by default, dark in apps that support it (Apple Mail, Outlook for Mac, iOS Mail). No web fonts or other outside resources are loaded beyond the logo. Account emails no longer link to notification settings and show the raw link under the button. Status page emails carry the page's name instead of the LogTide logo and use the page accent for the button when white text reads on it. Plain-text versions keep their content, with sentence-case headings like the HTML.
- **ClickHouse writes far less to disk**: on a hosted instance with about 3 GB of new data a day, ClickHouse was writing 300 to 500 GB a day, almost all of it background rewrites.
  - Inserts share a 2 second flush window instead of 200 ms, and trace summaries a 10 second one. Ingest answers up to about 2 seconds later; trace lists can show a new trace up to 10 seconds after its spans.
  - The logs and spans tables are partitioned by day instead of by month, so retention drops or rewrites single days instead of whole months.
  - The `proj_service_time` projection, a full second copy of every row, is removed. Log counts use the per-minute volume projection, now also created on ClickHouse 24.1.
  - The compose files mount `docker/clickhouse-system-logs.xml` and `docker/clickhouse-profile.xml`, which turn off the query profiler and the system log tables that grew forever. The old copies can be dropped by hand: see the comment at the top of `clickhouse-system-logs.xml`.

### Fixed

- **Retention did not apply to everything**: on ClickHouse and MongoDB, logs of organizations with the longest retention were never deleted, and on every engine spans, trace summaries and metrics ignored the organization retention. The nightly retention now covers all signals on every engine.
- **Project purge left trace summaries behind on ClickHouse**: they are now deleted with the spans.
- **Notification channel emails could carry HTML from the event**: the title and message of an email channel notification went into the HTML unescaped. They are escaped now.
